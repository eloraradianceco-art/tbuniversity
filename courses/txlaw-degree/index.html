Law: Texas (Family & Criminal Focus) — Four-Year Curriculum

Twenty-four courses, eight semesters. A law-school-style course of study built around Texas family law and criminal law, with the first-year core underneath: courts and legal method, constitutional law, contracts, torts, property, the Penal Code and criminal procedure, evidence, divorce, community property, support, conservatorship, juvenile justice, protective orders, civil practice, ethics and the bar. A study aid only. It is not legal advice, does not create an attorney-client relationship, and does not make anyone eligible to sit for the bar. Laws change; check the current statutes and consult a licensed Texas attorney about any real matter.
